01 — ABOUT

Sociological research grounded in data, context, and methodological rigor.

I am an MA student in Sociology at the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA). My dissertation investigates the Brazilian manosphere through a longitudinal corpus of public masculinist forums.

My trajectory brings together Social Sciences, Humanities, and digital methods. I work at UFBA’s Digital Humanities Laboratory (LABHDUFBA), with experience in corpus construction and preservation, textual and interaction-data analysis, visualization, and research infrastructure.

02 — RESEARCH

Research areas

01

Gender Studies and the Manosphere

Longitudinal study of masculinist communities, their repertoires, forms of belonging, criteria of authority, and discursive transformations.

02

Sociology of knowledge

Research on digital ecosystems, extremism, and the circulation of narratives. I am interested in mis/disinformation and its social impacts.

03

STS and Digital Methods

Corpus construction, web scraping, computational text analysis, networks, visualization, and reproducible research practices grounded in critical sociological thought.
